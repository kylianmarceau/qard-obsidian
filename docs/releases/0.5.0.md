Qard 0.5.0 adds exam planning and saved flashcard sessions.

- **Exam plans:** set an exam date, study weekdays and a daily review target for selected decks or topics. Qard spreads first-pass coverage across the remaining days, adds due reviews within your target, and warns when the workload exceeds available time. Open practice-test creation with your exam material already in the prompt.
- **Resume studying:** normal and cram sessions automatically preserve their card order and next position, including after restarting Obsidian. Resume from the deck library, the relevant deck, or the exam plan. Completed ratings and position updates save together.
- **Clearer layouts:** exam setup has spaced sections, responsive fields and grouped study days. Saved sessions show the deck name, progress, a Resume button and a separate discard control.
- **Workspace focus:** use F to toggle focus across the workspace and Escape to leave it.

Exam coverage counts cards reviewed since the plan began; it is not an exam-score prediction. Existing FSRS due dates remain unchanged. Cram sessions assign stable card IDs when needed for reliable resuming, without recording ratings or changing schedules.

Your existing notes, settings and review history are preserved.
