Qard 0.6.2 makes writing cards by hand faster, lets you turn off every AI feature with one switch, and lets you choose your own keys.

- **No more `Deck 2.md`:** **New card** now adds to the deck's existing note, after the last card of the topic or under its existing heading, instead of creating a new note every time. Cloze and image cards from the editor go into the same note. Below the fields, the editor shows which note and topic a card will join.
- **Add cards in a row:** **Add card** (or Ctrl/Cmd + Enter) saves and starts the next card with the same deck, topic and card type; **Done** opens the deck when you're finished. The topic field suggests the deck's topics, each topic in a deck has a **+** to start a card there, and the editor remembers your last card type, deck and topic.
- **Basic + reverse as a card type:** reverse pairs are now a card type next to Basic and Cloze, instead of an easy-to-miss checkbox.
- **Reverse existing cards:** **Add reverse** and **Remove reverse** in a card's preview, **Add reverse** for a selection of cards in a deck, and **Qard: Make card at cursor reversed** in a note.
- **New card in the sidebar:** **Qard: New card** opens the editor in the right sidebar, beside the note you're reading, starting on the deck and topic at the cursor. It stays open between cards. **Qard: Insert card** adds a card template in a note with the question selected.
- **AI features switch:** turn off **Settings → Qard → AI features** to hide tests, courses, lessons, AI generation, improvements, suggestions, token usage and AI settings, and to stop AI work. Nothing is deleted; turning it on brings everything back. Study time moved next to Statistics so it is reachable either way.
- **Choose your study keys:** remap Space, 1–4, U, E, S and F under **Settings → Qard → Study keys**. Rating keys also work by their position on the keyboard, so 1–4 rate on AZERTY without Shift. Every study action, plus Open decks and Open exam plans, is now a command you can bind in **Settings → Hotkeys**.

Notes written by earlier versions are left as they are; new cards go into the note with the most cards of their topic. Card IDs, review history and file formats are unchanged.

All 725 automated tests, formatting, type checking, source and stylesheet lint, build and release checks pass locally.

To install manually, download `main.js`, `manifest.json` and `styles.css` from this release and replace those three files in your vault's `.obsidian/plugins/qard/` folder. Reload Qard to apply the update. Keep `data.json`, the `study-backups` folder and your vault notes to preserve settings and study progress.
