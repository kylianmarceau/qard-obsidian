Qard 0.5.12 adds ways to improve, import and organize your flashcards while preserving existing notes and review history.

- **Improve a card with AI:** clarify a question, shorten its answer or split a basic card into smaller ones. Review and edit the proposal before applying it. Wording edits preserve scheduling; splits create new cards and pause the original with its history intact.
- **Import and export from settings:** preview CSV/TSV and Anki `.apkg` imports, including supported media, cloze cards and review data. Export all cards or one deck to CSV/TSV, and migrate Spaced Repetition cards, including standard clozes. Interrupted imports can be resumed.
- **Manage cards together:** select individual cards, whole topics or search matches to move, pause, resume or mark for repair. Action controls appear after selection. Moves preserve source notes, attachment links, stable IDs and review history.
- **Search questions and answers:** find cards by their answers as well as deck, topic, question and tags. Searching inside a deck shows the matching cards with their topics expanded.
- **Study desk navigation:** the review action opens Today, with one clear route to the cards ready to study.

To install manually, download `main.js`, `manifest.json` and `styles.css` from this release and replace those three files in your vault's `.obsidian/plugins/qard/` folder. Reload Qard to apply the update. Keep `data.json` to preserve your settings and study history.
