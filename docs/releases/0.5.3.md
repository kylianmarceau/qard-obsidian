Qard 0.5.3 adds more ways to make flashcards, new review controls, and a clearer session summary.

- **Cloze cards:** select a word or phrase and choose Hide selection to create numbered blanks. Add hints, preview each blank, and review them in normal or cram sessions.
- **Image occlusion:** draw masks over diagram labels or details. Review one mask at a time or hide them together; each card has its own review history.
- **Review controls:** undo the last rating, skip a card for the current session, or pause it until you are ready to study it again. Undo also restores the card's schedule and saved session position. Skipped and paused cards do not count as reviews.
- **Session summary:** a clean, borderless recall-difficulty curve shows your Again, Hard, Good, and Easy ratings, with counts, percentages, and a median marker. It includes ratings from resumed sessions and excludes skipped cards.

Your notes, settings, review history, saved sessions, and exam plans are preserved. All 404 automated tests and the formatting, TypeScript, source, stylesheet, build, and release checks pass.

To install manually, download `main.js`, `manifest.json`, and `styles.css` from this release and replace the files in your vault's `.obsidian/plugins/qard/` folder. Reload Qard to apply the update.
