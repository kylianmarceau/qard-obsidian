Qard 0.3.5 adds study statistics and adaptive FSRS scheduling.

- **Study statistics:** open Statistics in the workspace header or run Qard: Show study statistics. Explore your yearly activity heatmap, current and longest streaks, recall rate, rating-based difficulty, upcoming reviews, and results by deck.
- **FSRS-6 scheduling:** select FSRS under Settings → Qard → Study preferences → Review scheduler. It adapts intervals to your ratings and elapsed time, using ts-fsrs 5.4.2 with a default 90% target retention. Higher targets mean more frequent reviews. Personal parameter optimization is not included yet.
- **Memory estimates:** with FSRS enabled, see predicted recall, median stability, a model difficulty distribution, and the number of cards below your retention target. Cards with incomplete or imported history are labelled as approximate estimates.
- **Review interval previews:** reveal a flashcard to see the next review interval for Again, Hard, Good and Easy. Again means forgotten; Hard means recalled with effort.
- **Safe switching and complete history:** existing vaults keep the simple scheduler until FSRS is selected. Switching preserves current due dates and review counts. Complete saved histories initialize the memory model; new timestamped review events are retained without the previous 10,000-event limit. Previously removed history cannot be recovered.
- **Flashcard format update:** this release keeps standard question/answer Markdown cards and prompt-first AI generation. The experimental cloze and image occlusion formats from 0.3.4 have been removed; those experimental cards are not supported in 0.3.5.

Requires Obsidian 1.7.2 or newer. Update under Settings → Community plugins → Check for updates, then reload Qard after any running AI jobs finish. For a manual update, replace main.js, manifest.json and styles.css in your vault’s .obsidian/plugins/qard/ folder, keeping data.json to preserve your settings and study history.
