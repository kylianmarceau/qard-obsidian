Qard 0.5.23 keeps study progress safe when you study on more than one device, spreads out review dates, and shows when each card is next due.

- **Study on several devices:** when Obsidian Sync, iCloud or another sync service replaces Qard's `data.json` with a copy from another device, Qard now merges it instead of overwriting it. Ratings from every device are kept. A card rated on two devices before they synced is rescheduled from its combined ratings. Undone ratings, finished sessions and deleted exams stay removed. Pauses, repair markers and settings keep the most recent change. Study time and token usage are counted per device and added together, and resetting them holds on every device.
- **Spread-out reviews:** FSRS intervals of 2.5 days or more now vary slightly (about ±15% for a few days, narrowing to ±5% beyond 20 days), so cards added and rated together fall due on nearby days instead of all on the same day. The interval shown on a rating button is still the one saved. Learning steps and existing due dates are unchanged.
- **Next due date:** a card's preview shows when it is next due and how many times it has been reviewed.

Install this version on every device before relying on the merging. An older Qard version does not keep the new merge information when it saves. Merging was verified with simulated devices in automated tests; it has not yet been validated across two physical devices or on mobile hardware. Restoring a backup with less recorded study time than another device holds does not lower the total after the next merge.

All 638 automated tests, formatting, type checking, source and stylesheet lint, build and release checks pass locally.

To install manually, download `main.js`, `manifest.json` and `styles.css` from this release and replace those three files in your vault's `.obsidian/plugins/qard/` folder. Reload Qard to apply the update. Keep `data.json`, the `study-backups` folder and your vault notes to preserve settings and study progress.
