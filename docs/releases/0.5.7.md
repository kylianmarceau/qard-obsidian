Qard 0.5.7 lets you review individual due topics from Today.

- **Due topics by deck:** each deck now shows its due topics and card counts underneath. Topics without due cards stay hidden.
- **Choose what to review:** use **Study** beside a topic to review only that topic, or **Study all** beside the deck to review all its due cards. Topics with the same name in different decks stay separate, and reviews start with the oldest due cards.
- **Narrow layouts:** long deck and topic names wrap while keeping the Study actions visible.

To install manually, download `main.js`, `manifest.json` and `styles.css` from this release and replace those three files in your vault’s `.obsidian/plugins/qard/` folder. Reload Qard to apply the update.
