Qard 0.5.11 refines the deck library with a compact study desk and consistent workspace navigation.

- **Study desk:** a clean deck list keeps course colours, with today's due-card count and review actions alongside it.
- **Consistent tabs:** Decks, Tests, Learn and Plans stay aligned above the content, with matching spacing and underline positions on desktop and narrow screens.
- **Deck and topic browsing:** clicking a deck opens its topics and cards, with topic search and separate study and delete controls. Opening a deck from filtered search shows all its topics and preserves the search when returning.
- **Quieter visuals:** expanded topics no longer have a grey background, rating buttons remain neutral after revealing an answer, and the redundant vault footer is removed.

To install manually, download `main.js`, `manifest.json` and `styles.css` from this release and replace those three files in your vault's `.obsidian/plugins/qard/` folder. Reload Qard to apply the update.
