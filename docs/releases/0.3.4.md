Qard 0.3.4 adds cloze and image occlusion flashcards and makes AI generation start with your description.

- **Prompt-first AI flashcards:** the generation page now uses the same focused composer as New test. Describe what you want, optionally attach notes or whole folders, and choose Start. AI suggests a deck and topic after generation; you can adjust both while reviewing the cards.
- **Cloze cards:** create fill-in-the-blank cards using `{{answer}}` or `{{answer::hint}}`. Preview and study hide the answers until reveal. Toggle Cloze in the AI composer to draft these cards from your material.
- **Image occlusion cards:** choose a vault image and draw rectangular masks over labels or answers. Adjust the masks with numeric controls, then reveal the original image during study. All blanks or masks on a card share one review history and reveal together.
- **Reliable drafts and saves:** older AI drafts still reopen. Destination edits are saved with the draft, and the destination stays fixed once adding begins so save retries and later selections use the same note.

Requires Obsidian 1.7.2 or newer. Update Qard under **Settings → Community plugins → Check for updates**, then reload it after running AI jobs finish. For a manual update, replace `main.js`, `manifest.json` and `styles.css` in your vault's `.obsidian/plugins/qard/` folder, keeping `data.json`.
