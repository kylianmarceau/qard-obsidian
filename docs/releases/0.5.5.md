Qard 0.5.5 makes it easier to choose what to study and repair cards while reviewing.

- **Choose a deck on Today:** due cards are listed by deck with their own counts and Study actions. Each action starts a session containing only that deck’s due cards, oldest due first. After the final mastery check, Next returns to Today so you can choose a deck.
- **Edit during study:** choose Edit or press E to open the card editor without leaving the session. Saving returns to the same position with the answer hidden, keeping the card’s identity and review history.
- **Flag cards for repair:** Needs fixing is available under More actions during study and in card preview. Find flagged cards later with the Needs fixing filter on Decks, and use Mark fixed when a repair is complete.
- **Notice repeated failures:** Often forgotten appears after Again ratings on five different study days. The reminder suggests simplifying the question or adding an explanation. It is advisory; cards are never paused automatically.
- **Separate related cards:** after a scheduled normal review, other cloze or image variants from the same group are deferred until the next local midnight. Undo restores the deferral. Cram includes every variant, and Separate related cards can be disabled in settings.
- **Cleaner review controls:** Edit and More actions sit below the reveal/rating controls alongside Undo, Skip and Pause. The compact menu opens without moving the card, and repair reminders use less space.

Your notes, settings, review history, saved sessions and exam plans are preserved. Editing an answer uses the existing answer-changed check rather than resetting its history. New, paused, deferred and duplicate-ID cards are excluded from Today’s due counts.

All 450 automated tests and the formatting, TypeScript, source, stylesheet, build and release checks pass. Desktop and narrow layouts were verified with production components in an isolated preview.

To install manually, download `main.js`, `manifest.json` and `styles.css` from this release and replace those three files in your vault’s `.obsidian/plugins/qard/` folder. Reload Qard to apply the update.
