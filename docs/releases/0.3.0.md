Qard 0.3.0 brings the latest Test and Learn features to the main release, alongside AI flashcard generation.

- **AI flashcards:** choose Generate with AI from Decks or New card, request 1–100 cards, and attach notes or let the writer find relevant notes. Review, edit, preview and select cards before saving them into a deck.
- **Flashcard progress on the home page:** a Generating flashcards row shows the deck and card count, then changes to Review flashcards when ready. Review drafts and edits survive a plugin reload, and retrying a save does not duplicate cards.
- **Delete a test easily:** open a test and click the trash icon in the top bar. Its plan, questions and saved answers move to Obsidian's configured trash; any running jobs for the test stop.
- **Practice tests:** generate tests from prompts, notes and flashcard decks, review a plan first, answer questions and receive marking, answer reviews and suggested flashcards.
- **Learn:** map courses, work through guided lessons, check understanding and track mastery and due work.
- **AI connections and job controls:** use Claude Code, Codex, Anthropic or OpenRouter with separate Writer, Tutor and Marker settings. Track token usage, see background jobs, cancel stalled work and retry interrupted tasks.
- **Spaced Repetition import:** bring supported cards into Qard while retaining ordinary Markdown notes.

Requires Obsidian 1.7.2 or newer. AI features use your configured connection; manual flashcards and study remain available without one.

To update manually, download `main.js`, `manifest.json` and `styles.css` from this release and replace those three files in your vault's `.obsidian/plugins/qard/` folder. Once running AI jobs have finished, reload Qard. Keep `data.json` in place to retain your settings and review history.
