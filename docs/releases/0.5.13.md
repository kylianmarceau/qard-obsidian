Qard 0.5.13 adds optional study pacing, typed recall, maintained reverse cards and automatic progress backups, alongside lesson figures and study-time tracking.

- **Study at your pace:** set a daily new-card allowance and a review batch size under Settings → Qard → Study preferences. Both default to 0, preserving unlimited new cards and full sessions. Complete a batch and choose Continue studying for the remaining cards. Learning repeats do not consume the new-card allowance; cram and exam sessions remain unrestricted.
- **Optional typed recall:** enable Type answers before revealing in settings to write what you remember, reveal the answer, compare and choose your own rating. It defaults off, with no toggle on the flashcard screen. Ctrl/⌘ + Enter reveals; ordinary Enter supports multiline answers. Typed text is temporary, stays local and is not automatically graded.
- **Maintained reverse pairs:** select Also test the reverse when creating a basic card. Each direction has its own schedule and history; editing either updates the linked partner. The Separate related cards preference defers the other direction until tomorrow.
- **Automatic progress backups:** keep up to 30 dated local copies of review history, schedules, study time, saved sessions, exam plans and card/mastery links. Manage backups in settings, preview a restore and save a fresh safety copy before replacing progress. Restoring preserves current preferences and AI connections. Notes, attachments and AI drafts are separate vault files and are not included in these backups.
- **Lesson figures:** an Illustrator role can draw diagrams and plots for guided lessons. Ask for a figure with Draw this or request changes with Redraw. Figures are saved as reusable vault attachments; computed plots require desktop Python, with SVG diagrams available without it.
- **Study time:** view daily study time by activity and course from Learn or the Show study time command. Time is tracked locally while Qard is active, excluding idle periods.

This release also includes developer diagnostics and debug reports for troubleshooting. All 575 automated tests, formatting, type checking, source and stylesheet lint, build and release checks pass locally.

To install manually, download `main.js`, `manifest.json` and `styles.css` from this release and replace those three files in your vault's `.obsidian/plugins/qard/` folder. Reload Qard to apply the update. Keep `data.json`, the `study-backups` folder and your vault notes to preserve settings and study progress.
