Qard 0.4.0 adds source updates for generated flashcards and a fast cram mode.

- **Source updates:** generated flashcards, including cards made from tests and lessons, retain links to the notes they were based on. When those notes change, review the affected passages beside the card and ask your Writer connection for an editable update. Changes are never applied automatically.
- **Keep your history:** wording edits preserve the card's identity, history and schedule. If the correct answer changes, flag it for a fresh normal review without erasing previous reviews. Interrupted updates can be finished after a reload.
- **Existing cards:** open a card preview and choose Link a source note to start tracking an older or handwritten card. Source links follow note and folder renames while Qard is enabled; missing sources are flagged. Back up Qard/Source links.json with your notes and keep it at its original path.
- **Cram mode:** choose Normal session or Cram mode on the Study screen. In cram mode, Space reveals the answer and Space again advances. Touch controls provide the same flow. No difficulty ratings, review-history entries or schedule changes are made; Cram again repeats the selected cards.

Historical tests and lesson transcripts remain records of their original attempts. Source updates edit their derived flashcards, including the cards' answers and explanations.

Requires Obsidian 1.7.2 or newer. Update under Settings → Community plugins → Check for updates, then reload Qard after any running AI jobs finish. To update manually, replace main.js, manifest.json and styles.css in your vault's .obsidian/plugins/qard/ folder. Keep data.json to preserve settings and review history.
