Qard 0.5.8 brings learning repeats to normal FSRS deck study and makes completion progress accurate.

- **Missed cards return:** pressing **Again** in All, New or Difficult study keeps the card in the session and brings it back at its scheduled learning or relearning time, just as in Due study. Continue with other cards while waiting. New cards also return after Good when FSRS schedules another learning step.
- **Clear completion progress:** cards waiting to repeat do not count as completed. After forgetting the first card in a 52-card deck, progress stays at **0 / 52 completed · 1 learning**. Repeated attempts still count as reviews in Statistics.
- **Saved sessions:** completion counts stay accurate when leaving and resuming. Older saved deck sessions gain FSRS learning repeats when resumed, while preserving their card order and review history.

To install manually, download `main.js`, `manifest.json` and `styles.css` from this release and replace those three files in your vault’s `.obsidian/plugins/qard/` folder. Reload Qard to apply the update.
