Qard 0.3.3 lets AI choose how many flashcards your notes need and makes the plugin page easier to browse.

- **Automatic flashcard count:** choose your deck, topic and notes, then generate. The AI decides the number of cards needed to cover the important material without repetitive filler. There is no number selector or fixed 100-card limit.
- **Clear generation status:** the generation screen and Decks home page show that flashcards are being generated. The actual count appears when the cards are ready to review.
- **Saved drafts preserved:** existing drafts, edits, selections and added cards still reopen. Interrupted requests use automatic sizing when retried.
- **Shorter plugin page:** a concise overview of Decks, Tests and Learn, quick setup instructions, privacy information and screenshots of the study screen and course map. The full reference remains available in the linked guide.

Requires Obsidian 1.7.2 or newer. Update Qard under **Settings → Community plugins → Check for updates**, then reload it after running AI jobs finish. For a manual update, replace `main.js`, `manifest.json` and `styles.css` in your vault's `.obsidian/plugins/qard/` folder, keeping `data.json`.
