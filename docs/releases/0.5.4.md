Qard 0.5.4 brings learning and relearning cards back during normal Due cards sessions at their scheduled FSRS times.

- **Learning cards return:** cards still in learning, and forgotten cards in relearning, return between questions when they become due. The current question is never interrupted.
- **Wait or continue later:** when only future learning cards remain, the session shows the next return time. Keep it open to continue automatically, save and resume later, or finish early while preserving ratings and due dates. Saved learning queues survive a restart.
- **Review controls stay consistent:** undo restores the learning queue and the last rating. Skip or pause removes a returning card from the session. Progress counts distinct cards, and the summary also reports repeated reviews.

This applies to normal Due cards sessions with FSRS and scheduling enabled. Other study modes and previously saved single-pass sessions keep their existing behavior. Your notes, settings, review history, and exam plans are preserved.

All 425 automated tests and the formatting, TypeScript, source, stylesheet, build, and release checks pass.

To install manually, download `main.js`, `manifest.json`, and `styles.css` from this release and replace the files in your vault's `.obsidian/plugins/qard/` folder. Reload Qard to apply the update.
