Qard 0.3.1 adds easy deletion for decks, topics and learning material.

- **Delete a whole deck:** use the trash icon on the Decks page or beside the deck title. All cards in that deck are removed across its Markdown notes, including cards hidden by a search filter.
- **Delete a topic:** each topic has its own trash icon beside Study. Other topics and note content are preserved.
- **Delete Learn material:** remove courses and lessons from the Learn list, or courses, lessons and checks from their top bar. Removing a course moves its mastery file and associated generated lessons/checks to Obsidian's configured trash. Your original course notes, flashcards and practice tests stay.
- **Delete course mappings:** remove running mappings, failed mappings and proposals awaiting review. Deleted mappings no longer restart after reloading Qard.
- **Clear confirmation and reliable background deletion:** deletion explains its scope first. Relevant jobs stop, pending saves are drained, and late AI replies cannot recreate deleted learning items. Errors remain visible and can be retried.

Deck/topic deletion edits the matching flashcard callouts in place; it preserves the surrounding notes and cannot be undone from Qard. Learning files use Obsidian's configured trash.

Requires Obsidian 1.7.2 or newer. To update manually, download `main.js`, `manifest.json` and `styles.css` and replace those three files in your vault's `.obsidian/plugins/qard/` folder. Keep `data.json` to retain settings and review history, then reload Qard after running AI jobs finish.
