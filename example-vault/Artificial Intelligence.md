> [!qard]- What is Bayes’ theorem?
> $$
> P(A|B) = \frac{P(B|A)P(A)}{P(B)}
> $$
>
> Here $P(B)$ must be nonzero.

<!-- qard-id: example-rich-front -->
> [!qard]- Explain the following expression.
>
> $$
> E = mc^2
> $$
>
> What do its three symbols represent?
> <!-- qard-answer -->
> $E$ is energy, $m$ is mass, and $c$ is the speed of light in vacuum.
